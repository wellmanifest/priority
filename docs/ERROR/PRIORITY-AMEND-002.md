# PRIORITY-AMEND-002

Amendment without justification.

## Meaning

An amendment rewrites the intent itself, not merely its weight.

That is the one operation in the document capable of turning a priority into a different
priority while keeping its identity and history. It must say why, or the record of what
the intent used to be becomes unreadable.

## Cause

An amendment was added to record a change that had already been agreed elsewhere, so the reason felt redundant at the time of writing.

## Resolution

Add `BECAUSE "..."` saying why the intent itself changes, not merely why it became more urgent.
